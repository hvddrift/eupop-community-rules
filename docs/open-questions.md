# Open questions

Places where the rulebook text is unclear, or where we have not yet seen a confirmed answer. Each item should end up as a `consensus` or `confirmed` case, with a link to the discussion or ruling.

Rule refs are to the third printing (2026) rulebook.

| # | Question | Where | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | **"Outnumber 2:1": is exactly 2:1 enough?** With 8 land units vs 4, is that Partial Victory, or do you need 9+? Rulebook says "outnumber ... 2:1". **Answer (official, Player Aid B v1.3 (2026)):** the chart says "Either player has twice as many Land Units Deployed as their Enemy", so exactly 2:1 is enough. | p. 29 table | answered 🟢 |
| Q2 | **Does the 10 ducat Capital ransom count toward the 2× Tax Income cap** on ducats demanded? The cap text mentions "the return of a Realm's Capital and other Provinces". **Answer (official, Player Aid B v1.3 (2026), footnote 8):** "Loser can never be forced to pay more than 2 × Tax Income to Victor, **in total**." The same footnote is attached to the 10 Ducat Capital ransom and to the 3D per Tax Value demand, so they count together. | p. 30, Keep Current Board State | answered 🟢 |
| Q3 | **Partial Victory "separate peace with Active Allies: No (except when enforcing Secure Desired Succession)".** What exactly does that allow? Presumably SDS applies to the PR only, ignoring its allies ("Ignore Loser's Active Ally status") but please confirm. **Hint (rulebook p.31):** the Secure Desired Succession term itself says "Ignore Loser's Active Ally status", which supports that reading. Still no explicit ruling on the interaction with the table exception. | p. 29 table, p. 30 end of §10.3, p. 31 | partly answered 🔵 |
| Q4 | **Distant Continents footnote.** Units on Distant Continents where the other realm has no [icon] don't count toward 2:1. Which icon, and how is it counted in practice? **Answer (official, Player Aid B v1.3 (2026), footnote 5):** Units on Distant Continents where the enemy PR has no Town do not count. The icon is the Town icon. | p. 29 footnote | answered 🟢 |
| Q5 | **Partial Victory vs an NPR: what exactly is counted** ("the total number of [icon] in their Areas")? **Answer (damifoe, Discord, 4 Oct 2026, supported by the Invasion text):** the icon appears to be the Rebel Unit icon, and the count is Rebel Units in the **Victor's** Areas, plus Deployed Enemy Land Units (NPRs have none). See [PR-022](../cases/peace-resolution/022-partial-victory-vs-npr-rebel-units.md). Check the icon against the printed page. | p. 29 table | answered 🟡 |
| Q6 | **Surrender plus Humiliation without occupying the capital.** Allowed by the table footnote; does the Surrender Prestige swap (−2/+2) stack with Humiliation's Prestige exchange? | pp. 30–31 | open ([case 005](../cases/peace-resolution/005-surrender.md)) |
| Q7 | **Victor "must enforce" in Total Victory but several terms need extra conditions.** If the Victor can't satisfy any term except White Peace, are they forced into White Peace? **Partial answer (official, Player Aid B v1.3 (2026), footnote 6):** after a Surrender, "the Victor must Enforce a Peace that they can meet the requirements for". Total Victory itself is still not spelled out. **Related, unanswered (Discord, 29 Sep 2026):** if A resolves first, can A ask the other human player to **Surrender** during A's peace, or must they wait for their own turn? Eivind ruled that you **Enforce** only on your own turn ([PR-019](../cases/peace-resolution/019-enforce-on-your-own-turn.md)), but Miroku20X6 notes it isn't clear a Surrender must be on the surrendering player's own turn. | p. 29, p. 30 | open |
| Q8 | **White Peace as a term vs. the eligibility table.** The White Peace term text on p.30 is headed "Total Victory to Enforce" and says PRs involved lose 1 Prestige each, but the p.31 table allows White Peace for every result including Automatic White Peace. Which applies when, and does the Prestige loss apply to Automatic White Peace? **Answer (official, Player Aid B v1.3 (2026)):** each PR loses 1 Prestige per White Peace it enters, including an Automatic White Peace. | pp. 30–31 | answered 🟢 |
| Q9 | **Negotiated Peace: what does naming a Victor or Loser do?** The rules let players "agree on who will be named as the Victor or Loser, if anyone", but don't say what that changes (Prestige? Missions? Later terms?). **Answer (Mikhs, Discord #eu-general, 15 Oct 2026):** Some Peace Terms can only be applied to the Victor, so naming a Victor lets that player apply the effects of such a term. | p. 31 | answered 🟡 (needs message link) |
| Q10 | **Negotiated KCBS: how many Ducats for a returned Province?** The 3 Ducats per Tax Value rate and the 2× Tax Income cap are written for the Victor enforcing terms. Do they also apply when two players negotiate, or is the price free? **Hint (official, Player Aid B v1.3 (2026), footnote 8):** the cap is worded "can never be *forced* to pay", which fits the idea that it limits *enforced* payments, not ones the players freely agree. Still no explicit ruling. **More hints:** p.31 says that in a negotiated Keep Current Board State either party may exchange Occupied Provinces for Ducats, with **no rate stated**, and the Sept 2025 Discord thread (link to be added) treats everything as negotiated. Reading so far: likely **player-agreed**, but the amount rule is not explicit. | p. 30–31 | likely answered 🟡 (no explicit ruling) |
| Q11 | **Who is the "Active Player" for a Player-to-Player Diplomacy action inside a peace deal?** Monetary Support costs the *Active Player* 1 Diplomatic Power per 10 Ducats whether giving or receiving. Who pays when two players settle it in Peace Resolution? **Answer (Mikhs, Discord #eu-general, 15 Oct 2026):** Both players agree who pays; only one person pays for the action. | p. 15, p. 31 | answered 🟡 (needs message link) |
| Q12 | **Which Player-to-Player Diplomacy transactions can be bundled into a peace?** The peace text lifts the at-war restrictions, so Alliance, Royal Marriage and Buy/Sell Provinces look possible as well as Monetary Support. ## Icon legend (verify!)

The PDF text loses the icons. My reading of them from context: laurel with number = Prestige, ducat symbol = Ducats, shield = Claim. The crossed-tools icon on the ally tokens is the **Alliance** side of an ally token (§10.1 step 4: flip allied tokens "back to the A side"). **These need checking against the physical rulebook** before anyone relies on them. I deliberately avoided naming icons I couldn't identify.
