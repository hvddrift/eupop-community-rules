# Wars and Truces: where the tokens go

*Source: Discord thread "Question about war/peace token", 28 Mar 2026 (message link to be added). Rules: Declare War (Military Actions), §10.4 p.30, Defending the HRE §18.4 p.44.*

## War tokens
| Situation | Who places a War token, and where | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A PR declares war on a Realm | The **Aggressor** places their War token on the **Defender's Capital** | 🟢 Mikhs |
| You are Called to Arms by the **Aggressor** (Offensive CtA) | You also place your War token on the **Defender** | 🟢 Mikhs |
| You are Called to Arms by the **Defender** (Defensive CtA) | The **Aggressor** also places their War token on **your Capital** | 🟢 Mikhs |
| An NPR or DNPR declares war on a player through an **Event** | The **player** places their War token on the NPR (the one exception) | 🟢 Mikhs |
| Declaring war on two Realms (e.g. Bremen and Hamburg) | One token on each | 🟢 (two separate Realms) |

## Why it matters
Mostly it doesn't: you need to track **who is at war with whom**, **who has a Truce**, and **whether the war was offensive** (for the HRE). Do whatever keeps track of these (Atlas, Hubi).

The **one case where it matters** (Mikhs): **Defending the HRE**. The Emperor's ability covers **external Aggressors**, meaning players with a War token on the **Emperor's Capital**. It does **not** cover Realms the Emperor himself declared war on. While Defending the HRE is tagged, external Aggressors can't resolve peace separately with NPR Imperial Subjects unless they have a Total or Partial Victory over the Emperor (§18.4, p.44; also summarised in the [peace overview](../peace-resolution/00-overview.md)).

## Truces (§10.4, p.30)
- After peace, War tokens flip to the Truce side until Phase 3 of the next Round.
- **No Truce on Active Allies of former enemies, and never on Vassals.** War/Truce tokens are removed from Realms that have been Vassalized.
- **Truces are bidirectional** (🟡 Atlas). Neither side can declare war on the other, or on the other's NPR Allies or Vassals, whichever Capital holds the token.
- A **CB token ignores a Truce**.
- Subtle (🟡 Atlas): the wording is slightly different from "if you were at war last Round, you have a Truce this Round". If you **Cut Ties with an NPR Ally during the Truce Round**, only you get a Truce with that NPR, not everyone you have a Truce with.

## Restrictions on Declaring War (p.22, paraphrased) 🔵
You may not Declare War: on an Ally; on a Realm you have a Truce with (unless you use a General CB token); on a PR that has Passed; on an NPR Ally of a PR you have a Truce with or who has Passed; on certain HRE Members at peace with the Emperor (if he is your Ally, has a Truce with you, or has Passed); on Distant Realms you have no CB for; or during an Interregnum. Exceptions: a Marriage showing the Disputed Succession side lets you Declare War on an Ally or during an Interregnum; you can ignore every restriction when answering a Defensive CtA; and an Event that triggers a war states its own exceptions. The list doesn't say "a Realm you are already at war with", but Mikhs ruled you can't (🟢, 11 Oct 2026).
