# Naval

*Rules: Naval Battles §9.9, NPRs defending Sea Zones p.36, Naval Movement p.25, naval Retreat p.28. Sources: Discord, Oct 2026 (links to be added).*

## Naval Battle when an NPR ally joins a war (Hanbo, 12 Oct 2026)
France declares war on England and calls its NPR ally Burgundy (with a Dutch Vassal). England has Light Ships in a Sea Zone next to those Ports. 🟢 Mikhs: **yes, a Naval Battle triggers**, even though England is the target and not the Declarer. §9.9's fourth bullet applies: "PR Ships are located in a Sea Zone where, upon a DoW, one or more NPR Ports become Hostile to the PR". The p.36 text is worded for the Declarer, but Mikhs says it's not relevant. 🔵 Exception: no Battle if Ships hostile to that NPR were already there before the current Turn. The NPR fights with Light Ships equal to its NC for that Sea Zone. My reading (🔵, §13.2): the Dutch Vassal's Ports count as part of Burgundy's Realm during war.

## Moving into a Port (MangoRider, 10 Oct 2026)
🟢 Mikhs: moving into a Port is a move, so it uses one of the 2 spaces. Two Sea Zones plus a Port is not possible. 🔵 p.25: a Ship moves up to 2 spaces (Sea Zones or Ports).

## Retreating into a Port 🔴 open ([Q21](../open-questions.md))
🟢 Mikhs: you can only retreat to **adjacent spaces**, so a fleet that fought in the Western Mediterranean can retreat to a non-Hostile Sea Zone but "not to the Ports facing the Western Med Sea Zone". But the naval Retreat text (p.28) says Ships may retreat to an adjacent non-Hostile Sea Zone **or into an adjacent Friendly Port**, and the Tactical Retreat card also says "(or Friendly Port)". How to square them is unclear, and MangoRider's own comment (a defender retreating into Port to repair Heavy Ships) suggests Port retreat is possible in some case. Needs clarification.
