# Missions and Events

## Dominate Trade: the 2 Light Ships (Friedrich, 15 Oct 2026)

*Card: Own 4+ Ports adjacent to a Maritime Trade Node where you occupy all Trade Protection slots. Effect: gain 2 Light Ships adjacent to the Node.*

**Question:** can you take the reward in a Distant Continent sea zone where you have no Claim, Town or subjects? Distant Continents normally need an Explore Action to enter.

- 🟡 **Miroku20X6:** Missions follow the **Event Guidelines** (p.40). Under "Gain Military Unit", place the Units in a location where **no Battle is triggered**, and if none is eligible you don't gain them. The Distant Continent **movement** restrictions don't apply, since nothing is moving. Atlas agrees it's allowed, "but extremely unlikely to happen".
- 🟣 **Mikhs (opinion):** "While the rules don't specify it, I am of the opinion that you should only be able to gain ships following the Recruit Units requirements", meaning in Ports or non-hostile Sea Zones adjacent to them. Atlas's groups often house-rule it that way.
- **How can the Sea Zone be unexplored?** Atlas: you used Explore to put Ships there but placed no Discovery token, or you colonized one spot and lost it to Rebels after already occupying the slots.
- **Not answered:** what happens to your Ships there if your Town or Vassal is later conquered.

See [Q17](../open-questions.md).

## Grand Coalition II (461B) when you're already at war with the rival (arwweks, 14 Oct 2026)

- 🟢 Mikhs: you can't Declare War on a Realm you are at war with.
- 🟡 damifoe: you can still play the Event and name that Realm as the rival. Only the Declare War part doesn't happen, and the other players still pick sides and gain the Military cubes.
- 🟣 Mikhs: "I think that you must pick a target to which you can Declare War." damifoe would play it that way, but says it isn't written that way. Mikhs suggested rewording the card so the Prestige and Military rewards come with the Declare War.
- **Unasked:** the card says to remove all War tokens and Truces between players on opposite sides *before* it says to Declare War. If the Bots count as players, naming France or Castile as the rival might remove the existing War token and make the Declare War possible. Nobody checked this.

See [Q18](../open-questions.md).

## Novgorod scenario cards (debr41 and others, Jul to Oct 2026)

*Eclipse the Grand Prince (mission 3A) and The Fate of Veliky Novgorod (229-2).*
- 🟡 damifoe: "Gain 3 [icon] on 1-Tax **Realms**" includes **Vassals** of NPRs, because the card says Realms and Vassals are Realms but not NPRs.
- 🟡 Finn: when you "play on as" another Realm (Muscovy or Lithuania) you **don't get new Missions** (they belong to the scenario setup) but you do get that Realm's events. damifoe agrees.
- 🟡 Mikhs (guess): you probably change your Capital, the same way your Cores change. Not specified in the rules ([Q20](../open-questions.md)).
- 🟡 Finn, endorsed by Mikhs: you **don't place Towns** on Lithuanian Cores you don't own at the switch. Finn would make the remaining Provinces a **DNPR** to show that on the board (🟣 table suggestion).
- The card shows both options labelled "B)". The first should presumably be A).
