# Religion

*Rules: Convert Area p.14, Religious Dissent pp.9 and 38, Spread of Religious Ideas p.41, Eligible Marriage Partners p.32, Force Conversion p.31. Sources: Discord, Oct 2026 (links to be added).*

## Converting an Area
- 🔵 **Convert Area (2 Admin + 3 Ducats, p.14):** you may convert an Area's religion to your State Religion if you **Own every Province** in the Area(s) governed by its Religion slot, **or** you own at least one and all the rest are Owned by Realms with the same State Religion as yours. It adds Unrest to one of your affected Towns and you roll Rebel Dice.
- 🔵 **Development card (ADM-02-1):** "Use in Areas where you Own all Provinces" applies to all three options, including Missionaries (card text; Bogdan, 5 Oct 2026). So you can't use it in an Area you only partly own.
- 🟡 Bao: an NPR's State Religion is the religion of its **Capital Area** (exceptions on the Realm Reference Charts). Example: Poland could convert Orthodox Areas where Poland and Lithuania both have Provinces, since both are Catholic. The Ottomans couldn't convert the Hungarian Plains unless they change Hungary's religion, for example through war.
- Card design chatter (the opening line is easy to miss) is a suggestion for a reprint, not a rule.

## Religious Dissent (Phase 3, Step G) 🔵 with 🟡
The Town part (Unrest on 1 of your Towns in each Area with Dissent) and the Vassal part (in **1 affected Area**, remove 1 Influence or add Unrest to 1 Vassal token) are **resolved separately**, so one Area can take both. The Vassal part is **at most 1 per Round in total**, not per Vassal Realm (🟡 Miroku20X6, Oct 2026).

## Marriage and religion
- 🔵 **Who you can marry (p.32):** Christian PRs, other Christian Realms. Muslim PRs, other Muslim Realms. Revolutionary PRs, no other Realms. The only explicit removal trigger is an NPR you're Married to becoming another Realm's Vassal.
- 🔴 **Do existing Marriages end when you change religion?** Not settled ([Q22](../open-questions.md)). Mikhs and sim-h lean towards keeping them (the rules govern *making* a Marriage). Atlas thinks "eligible" lightly implies they should go, and removes them at his table. Miroku's analogy: a converted Emperor stays Emperor but isn't eligible at the next election, so treat Marriages the same way (keep them, make no new ones). Nobody with authority has ruled.
- If Hungary becomes Muslim by a legal route (for example Force Conversion), the Ottomans can Marry it (🟡 Bogdan).
