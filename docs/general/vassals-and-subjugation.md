# Vassals and Subjugation

*Rules: Vassals §11.4 p.33, Subjugate p.33, NPRs with Vassals §13.2 p.35, Marriages p.32, Owned p.28. Sources: Discord, Oct 2026 (links to be added).*

## Control (Force Conversion, Debr41, Oct 2026)
Force Conversion needs the Victor to **Control all Provinces in the Loser's Capital Area**. 🟢 Mikhs: **Controlled means Owned or Occupied**. A Vassal's Province is neither, so having the Livonian Order as a Vassal and only Riga occupied doesn't satisfy it. Total Victory doesn't waive the Area requirement. See [PR-020](../../cases/peace-resolution/020-force-conversion-control.md).

## Subjugating an NPR with Vassals (Borek, 11 Oct 2026)
- 🔵 A Marriage is required to Vassalize an NPR that has its own Vassals. Its Vassals become yours only if you also discard Influence from **their** Areas equal to **their** Base Tax. Otherwise they are released as NPRs (you may place an Alliance token on their Capital or a Claim in 1 of their Areas).
- 🟢 Mikhs: you can't count Influence from the Vassal's Area (Wielkopolska) towards subjugating Bohemia. The Influence must be in the target's **own** Areas. The Marriage is required "regardless if you also Vassalize their Vassal".
- 🟡 TheNegativeTwo: you can't Subjugate an NPR with a Vassal in any capacity without a Marriage. The "released as NPRs" rule applies only if you're Married and don't pay for the Vassals.
- 🔵 p.32: a Marriage counts as an Influence for all purposes. 🟡 Miroku's reading: that covers part of the requirement, so you need one fewer cube.

## Running out of Vassal tokens (debr41, 12 and 14 Oct 2026)
🟡 Mikhs (he says no rules cover it): you can Vassalize as much as your tokens allow and leave the rest as an NPR. There is no choice to take less. You still need Influence equal to the NPR's **total** Tax Value plus opponent Influence, and discard it all. The NPR gets a new Capital if its old one was Vassalized. He suggests moving any Marriage to the new Vassal, wherever its Capital is. You can leave the Capital out ("no rules at all"). Does the same logic apply to the **Vassalization peace term**? Not covered ([Q19](../open-questions.md)).
