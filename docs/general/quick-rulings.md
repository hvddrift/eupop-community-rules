# Quick rulings

One-line answers collected from Discord threads in 2026 (message links still to be added, except where shown). Status key as in the main README: 🟢 designer or developer, 🟡 experienced players, 🔵 rulebook text, 🟣 houserule, 🔴 open.

| Topic | Ruling | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Generals | A General whose army is wiped out **stays assigned** to that army. Reassign him with Appoint General, or wait for new Units to be Recruited into it. (Borek, 15 Oct 2026) | 🟢 Mikhs; Appoint General p.13 |
| Tactical Retreat (MIL-11-2) | "And/or" means you can use **both** effects or **either one**, including only the Ill Health removal. (12 Oct 2026) | 🟢 Mikhs |
| Centralization of Power (32A-3) | The A/B choice is **mandatory** for a PR who meets the condition. Only drawing the 2 Action Cards is optional. (14 Oct 2026) | 🟢 Mikhs |
| Offensive Call to Arms | Costs **2 Influence per NPR Ally** called, even if allies share an Area. Defensive costs 1 per NPR Ally. PR allies are free but may refuse. (10 to 15 Oct 2026) | 🟢 Mikhs; see [known issues](../known-issues.md) |
| Declaring war | You can't Declare War on a Realm you are **already at war with** (🟢 Mikhs, 11 Oct 2026), nor on a player who has **Passed** or their **NPR Allies** (🔵 §4.2 p.7, p.22). An Event doesn't bypass these unless it says so (🔵 p.22). Calling a PR ally offensively is **one** Declaration of War. Only the **Aggressor** removes Influence from the target's Towns and Vassals. | 🟢 / 🔵 |
| Disputed Succession (12A-2) | You can't use its free Declare War on a Passed player or their NPR Allies. The rule covers NPR **Allies**, not Marriage partners, so check whether an Alliance token is actually there. (13 Oct 2026) | 🟡 Finn; 🔵 p.22 |
| Events that grant an Alliance or Marriage | Only the **target** Realm must be Independent and at Peace. The recipient has no say. "Enters" means the same as "gain". (4 Oct 2026) | 🔵 Event Guidelines; 🟡 damifoe; 🟢 Mikhs |
| Rebels and Island Provinces | Rebels Siege Island (blue Port) Provinces **without Ships**. Defensive Mentality **applies** to a sieging Rebel army, but **not** to Rebellion rolls. (BGG, 13 May 2023, [thread](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3081142/can-rebels-always-siege-island-provinces)) | 🟢 Eivind Vetlesen |
| Mission rewards | Follow the **Event Guidelines** (p.40). See [Missions and Events](missions-and-events.md). | 🔵 |
| Colonists | Only Claims on **empty Territories** grant Colonists, and you gain them in **Phase 5**, not during Explore. (Oct 2026) | 🟡 Miroku20X6, Phil |
| Religious Dissent (Phase 3, Step G) | The Town part and the Vassal part resolve **separately** (you can "double dip" an Area). The Vassal part is **at most 1 Unrest per Round in total**, not per Vassal Realm. (Oct 2026) | 🟡 Miroku20X6; 🔵 p.9, p.38 |
| Spreading religions | Alternate between the religions **if possible**. It's fine to spread one religion 4 times, or 3 and 1, if the other has 0 or 1 clusters. Auto-resolution follows the same pattern. | 🟡 Miroku20X6 (check the printed text) |
| NPR State Religion | The religion of its **Capital Area**, unless the Realm Aid Sheet lists an exception at game start (for example Bohemia is Catholic though its Area shows Diverse Faiths). (2023, Oct 2026) | 🟡 Atlas, Rafiki, Bao |
| Control | **Controlled** means you Own it or Occupy it. A Vassal's Province is neither. (Oct 2026) | 🟢 Mikhs; 🔵 p.28 |
| Elector Area, Vassalized | Still an Elector Area. It votes for its **Overlord** if eligible. (13 Oct 2026) | 🟢 Mikhs; 🔵 p.45 |
| Naval movement | Moving into a Port is a **move**, so 2 Sea Zones plus a Port is not possible. (10 Oct 2026) | 🟢 Mikhs; 🔵 p.25 |
| Naval Battle when an NPR ally joins | An NPR ally called into a war can trigger a Naval Battle against Ships of the **target** PR too. No Battle if hostile Ships were already there. (12 Oct 2026) | 🟢 Mikhs; 🔵 §9.9 |
| Grand Coalition when already at war with the rival | **Open.** See [Q18](../open-questions.md). | 🔴 |
