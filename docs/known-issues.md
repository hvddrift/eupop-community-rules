# Known issues and printing differences

Things that are unclear, inconsistent or worded differently across sources. Not rules, just flags.

## Player Aid B (Peace Resolution flow chart, v1.3, 2026)
- **"Active Player" box and "Either player has twice as many Land Units".** The box that picks the Active Player ("first player in the overall Turn order who is involved in an ongoing War") should reference the previous node, which limits the pick to a PR who **wants to end** a war. With that fix the "Either player" box is not a problem (🟢 Eivind, designer, 22 Sep 2026). In practice: you **Enforce only on your own turn**. See [PR-019](../cases/peace-resolution/019-enforce-on-your-own-turn.md).
- Čačka (Jun 2026) first thought the chart misrepresented §10.1 on Total Victory timing, then withdrew that. See [PR-012](../cases/peace-resolution/012-total-victory-after-partial-victory-resolves.md).

## Rulebook wording by printing
- A Discord screenshot of §10 (Sep 2026) reads: "During Peace Resolution, Vassals are treated as part of their Overlord's Realm **for all purposes**" and "a PR with Active Allies normally **negotiates** on behalf of their Active Allies". Our **third printing (2026)** copy reads "for **Peace Resolution** purposes" and "normally **resolves Peace** on behalf of". The meaning looks the same. §13.2 (p.35) still says "for all purposes during War and Peace Resolution" for NPR Vassals. If you have an earlier printing, tell us which.
