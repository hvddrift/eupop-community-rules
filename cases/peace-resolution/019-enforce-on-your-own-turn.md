# You Enforce Peace Terms only on your own turn

## The question
If your opponent can enforce terms on you, does that happen during **your** turn or **theirs**?

## Answer (🟢 Eivind, designer)
In step 3 the paragraph that begins "Where a player has met the Partial Victory requirements…" refers to the **Active Player**, like the paragraphs before it. So you can **Enforce only on your own turn** in Turn Order. You may still **negotiate** on your own turn (damifoe, Phil, Miroku20X6).

## The disagreement
Atlas argued the text didn't say so, because "resolve" can include being enforced upon. The designer settled it on 22 Sep 2026.

## Chart caveat
Player Aid B's "Either player has twice as many Land Units" box can look as if the other side may enforce on your turn. Eivind agreed the box circled red in the discussion ("first player in the overall Turn order who is involved in an ongoing War is selected as Active Player") should reference the previous node, which limits the pick to a PR who wants to end a war. See [known issues](../../docs/known-issues.md).

## Table houserule (🟣 not official)
Atlas allows negotiation to happen **twice**, so players can react to resolutions that happen between their turns.
