# Provinces of an Active Ally under Keep Current Board State

## The question
A PR's Active Ally NPR has Provinces that are Occupied. Under Keep Current Board State, who pays the Ducats when they are returned: the NPR, or the PR ally from its own treasury?

## What the rulebook says (🔵, p.30)
- **One Peace Term is chosen for all the Realms involved** (the PR and its Active Allies), and is resolved as described for allies.
- With Keep Current Board State, Provinces Lawfully Owned by an enemy's Active Allies are treated mostly like any other Provinces you Occupy. You may keep them as normal (**except Capitals**), or **return them to get Ducats in return**.
- You **cannot Liberate** an Active Ally's Provinces to gain Prestige.
- With Humiliation, Prestige is only for Provinces returned to PR enemies directly. Provinces Owned by Active Allies are returned with **no** Prestige reward.
- For Vassalization and Force Conversion, apply the effects only to the Realms for which the Victor meets all the extra requirements.

## What the community said
- 🟡 **Miroku20X6:** the PR ally selects the Peace Term, and it is applied to the PR and its Active Ally **separately**. Terms that only fit one realm apply only to that realm, and the others get a **White Peace**. "So it's always a single peace term, but it is resolved separately for the two realms if applicable." He pointed to the "only a single Peace Term may be selected for all the Realms involved" sentence.
- **Who pays:** Miroku first said "from the NPR itself, but subject to the usual NPR restriction" (he posted an image that isn't in our copy; it may be the 2 × Tax Income cap note). Borek said that as far as he could tell the **player** pays, not the NPR. Nobody settled it.
