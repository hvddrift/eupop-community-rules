# Total Victory over an NPR ally changes the war with its PR

## Setup
| | |
|---|---|
| France | PR |
| Scotland | NPR, **Active Ally of France** |
| England | PR, at war with France and Scotland |
| Board | France sieges **nothing**. England sieges **all of Scotland**. |

## The question
In what order do the steps resolve, and does England have to make peace with France?

## Resolution, step by step
1. **Automatic White Peace?** No. France's side includes Scotland (an Active Ally counts as part of France's realm), and England occupies Scotland's Provinces.
2. **Total Victory step.** England has a Total Victory over **Scotland**, and **must** resolve it now. Peace with Scotland **removes the Alliance** with France, even if Scotland still exists.
3. Now France and England occupy nothing of each other's, so they **would** meet White Peace conditions. But that war **was not** forced to resolve in step 2.
4. **Remaining wars.** France and England **do not have to** make peace now. If they want to, they may **negotiate**, or enforce if one has twice the other's Land Units. They are not forced into White Peace, nor into any peace at all.

## Why (the intent)
Each step resolves in Turn Order, and one resolution can change another war. Step 2 is mandatory, step 3 is optional (Atlas's wording; 🟡).
