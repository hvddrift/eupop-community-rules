# Partial Victory and the 2:1 threshold

## Setup
Player A and Player B are both human PRs, at war. Neither has been eliminated. Counting **Deployed Land Units** at Peace Resolution:

| | A's units | B's units | Result? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scenario 1 | 9 | 4 | |
| Scenario 2 | 5 | 4 | |
| Scenario 3 | 8 | 4 | |

## The question
At what point does A reach Partial Victory against a human Enemy?

## Resolution
- Table p.29: human Enemy → "PR's Deployed Land Units **outnumber** the Deployed Land Units of their Enemy 2:1, or the Enemy Surrenders."
- **Scenario 1 (9 vs 4):** 9 is more than double 4. **Partial Victory** under any reading.
- **Scenario 2 (5 vs 4):** not close to 2:1. **No Partial Victory.** If nobody occupies any Provinces it is Automatic White Peace; otherwise Inconclusive.
- **Scenario 3 (8 vs 4):** exactly 2:1. **Disputed:** "outnumber 2:1" could mean "at least twice as many" (Partial Victory) or "more than twice as many" (not yet). See [Q1](../../docs/open-questions.md).

## Status notes
Scenarios 1 and 2 are clear. Scenario 3 needs a designer or community ruling. Please link it here once there is one.
