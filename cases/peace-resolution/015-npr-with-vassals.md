# Peace against an NPR that has Vassals

## The question
You occupy **all of Burgundy's own Provinces but none of its Vassals'**. Is it a Total or only a Partial Victory?

## Answer
1. **Partial.** For Peace Resolution, Vassals are treated as part of their Overlord's Realm (§10 intro p.29; for NPRs also §13.2 p.35: "for all purposes during War and Peace Resolution"). Total Victory needs **all** of the enemy's de jure Provinces, so the Vassals' Provinces count as Burgundian too (🟡 Miroku20X6, quoting that sentence).
2. **Keep Current Board State** then means returning Burgundy's **Capital** (ransom, see [PR-006](006-keep-current-board-state-options.md)).
3. **Vassalization** is the other option. 🟡 Miroku: it also vassalizes the Vassals. This fits the term text (v on "all of the Loser's de jure Provinces") together with the Vassals-count rule, but no designer has confirmed it.
4. **Unrest:** add it where the term says so. insearchofescapism: **only in Areas where you Occupied nothing**, not on every Town you didn't Occupy (matches the p.31 wording).
5. Atlas: all Burgundian Vassals have Tax 2, so annexing each takes one **Subjugate** card.

## You did not need to occupy everything
Vassalization needs only a **Partial or Total Victory**, an **NPR Loser**, your occupation of the **Capital**, and at least one de jure Province in **another Area** (if any) (p.31). Bao happened to hold all of them.
