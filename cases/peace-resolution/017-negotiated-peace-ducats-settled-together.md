# Ducats in one peace are settled together

## The question
In a Negotiated Peace where Provinces are exchanged, do you pay the **10 Ducats for returning the Capital first** (and possibly go bankrupt) before the rest of the deal?

## Answer (🟡 Miroku20X6)
Treat the deal as **simultaneous** and net it. **Example (invented numbers):** A returns the Capital for **−10** and two Provinces for **+6**, so the net is **−4**, applied once. It is not an instant −10 that triggers bankruptcy followed by +6.

## Why
- Miroku's reason: the total Ducats paid (Capital and Provinces) are factored together when working out the maximum payout (cf. Player Aid B footnote 8, "in total", [PR-006](006-keep-current-board-state-options.md)).
- Supporting text (🔵): p.31 says the Cut Ties or Diplomacy Action and the Peace Terms are "settled as one agreement, and not separately". That sentence is about the Action, but it shows the intent that a peace deal is one package.

## Status notes
Five thumbs-up and the asker agreed, but there is no designer ruling. This is the recommended table procedure, not an official ruling.
