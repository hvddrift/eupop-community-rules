# One sieged Province, inconclusive war: the menu of outcomes

*Started from a Discord post. The idea: for a common situation, list **every** way it can end, so players can see the options side by side.*

## Setup
| | |
|---|---|
| Player A | Ottomans (human PR) |
| Player B | Venice (human PR) |
| Board | The Ottomans **Occupy one Venetian Province, Crete**. Nothing else is occupied. No Capital is occupied. |
| Armies | Neither side's Deployed Land Units outnumber the other's 2:1 |
| Crete | Tax Value **T** (Hanbo's example used 3 Ducats, which fits T = 1; check the board) |

## The question
The war is stuck. What are all the ways Peace Resolution can end it?

## First, what kind of result is this?
- Not an **Automatic White Peace**: the Ottomans do occupy a Venetian Province (table p.29).
- Not a **Partial** or **Total Victory**: no 2:1, no Surrender, Crete is not all of Venice (table p.29).
- So it is an **Inconclusive Result**: *White Peace* or *Negotiated Peace* (human enemy). **Nobody can enforce terms, unless a player Surrenders** (§10.2, p.29).
- Both sides must agree, because Peace Terms against a human player must be negotiated (§10.1 step 3).
- **Naming a Victor matters:** some Peace Terms can only be applied *by the Victor*. Naming a Victor in a Negotiated Peace is what lets that player apply the effects of such a term (Mikhs). Without a named Victor, you are limited to the terms that don't need one (White Peace, and the Keep Current Board State exchange both sides may use).
- With only Crete occupied, the Capital-based terms (Force Conversion, Secure Desired Succession) are out. Vassalization and Full Annexation are never available in a Negotiated Peace or against a human (p.31).

## The menu

| # | Outcome | Crete ends up with | Prestige / money | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **White Peace** | Venice (returned) | **Both lose 1 Prestige** | p.30 |
| **2** | **Keep Current Board State, as it stands** | **Ottomans** keep it | Nothing changes hands. The Ottomans' token stays; Venice's is gone. | p.30 |
| **3** | **Keep Current Board State, Ottomans return Crete for Ducats** | Venice | **Venice pays the Ottomans Ducats.** Amount is whatever you agree: 3 Ducats if T = 1 at the Victor's rate of 3 per Tax Value. | p.31 ("either party may exchange Occupied Provinces for Ducats") |
| **4** | **Option 2 plus Monetary Support** (Ottomans keep Crete *and* pay Venice) | Ottomans | Ottomans pay Venice Ducats via Player-to-Player Diplomacy. The paying player spends **1 Diplomatic Power per 10 Ducats, rounded up**, so up to 10 Ducats for 1. | p.15, p.31 |

### Add-ons: one Cut Ties and/or one Player-to-Player Diplomacy action
Any of 1 to 3 can be combined with **one** of these, "paying for the Action as normal" (p.31). It is settled together with the term as one agreement:
- **Monetary Support** (that is option 4 above).
- **Royal Marriage** or **Alliance**: 1 Diplomatic Power each, tokens on each other's Capital (p.15).
- **Buy/Sell Provinces**: price 3 to 15 Ducats per Province. The rulebook says you can't mix this with Monetary Support in the same turn (p.15).
- **Cut Ties**: return Influence/Control tokens and/or end Alliances (with the usual penalties) (p.14).

**Community answer (Mikhs, Discord, 15 Oct 2026):** these Player-to-Player Diplomacy actions **can** be bundled into a Negotiated Peace. The options become possible *because you are no longer at war once the peace is concluded*, which is why the at-war restriction on p.15 doesn't block them.

**Who pays the Diplomatic Power?** For the actions that involve a payment (such as Monetary Support), only one player pays the Diplomatic Power, and **both players agree who that is** (Mikhs).

### If someone Surrenders
Surrender is the only way to *enforce* terms on an Inconclusive war.
- **Venice Surrenders:** Venice loses **2 Prestige**, the Ottomans gain **2** (p.30). The Ottomans may enforce terms **as if they had a Partial Victory**, and **must end the war this Round**, by an enforced term or a Negotiated Peace (p.30). Enforceable terms here:
  - **Humiliation**: allowed *without* occupying the Capital because Venice surrendered. All occupied Provinces return; the Ottomans score **2 × Tax Value returned to Venice (max 10)**, Venice loses the same. For Crete that is **2T**, so 2 Prestige if T = 1 (p.30).
  - **Keep Current Board State**, enforced: Ottomans keep Crete, **or** return it and demand **3 Ducats per Tax Value** (3T), capped at **2 × Venice's Tax Income** after returns, **or** swap it for a Province of equal Tax Value that Venice holds (p.30).
  - Not available: Full Annexation (Partial Victory can't), Vassalization/Force Conversion/Secure Desired Succession (need the Capital).
- **Ottomans Surrender:** Venice becomes the Victor, and the Ottomans lose 2 Prestige to Venice's 2 (p.30). Venice may enforce Humiliation without holding the Ottoman Capital (Player Aid B, footnote 6). Crete returns to Venice. But Humiliation's Prestige is 2 × the Tax Value of Provinces returned *to the Loser*, and Venice occupies nothing of the Ottomans', so that part scores **0**. Only the 2-point Surrender swap changes hands.
- Only **human** players can Surrender, and the Victor must enforce a peace they can meet the requirements for (Player Aid B, footnote 6).

### If nobody agrees
The war simply continues. You are never forced to end a war in step 3 (§10.1). **Truce** only starts once a peace is concluded (§10.4).

## After any peace
Truce token between Ottomans and Venice until Phase 3 of the next Round; units in Neutral Areas relocate; allies' tokens flip back (§10.1 step 4, §10.4).

| b) Keep board state, Venice keeps the location, Venice pays 3D | ✔ Menu **3**. The *amount* isn't fixed by the rules for a negotiated deal (Q10). |

| d) Keep board state, Ottomans keep it, Monetary Support via PtP, −1 Dip, up to 10D | ✔ Menu **4**. Correct cost: 1 Dip per 10 Ducats, rounded up. Who pays the Dip is unclear (Q11). |

Missing from the list: the other **Player-to-Player add-ons** (marriage, alliance, buying provinces, Cut Ties), the **Surrender** routes, and the option to **keep fighting**.

## Further notes from the community
- **Liberation is allowed in a negotiated Keep Current Board State.** p.31: both sides may agree to Liberate Provinces **if they give up all Prestige gains from Liberation** (🔵 rulebook text).
- **A Victor can matter** for terms that only a Victor can use. Mikhs said this also affects what a named Victor can apply (see above). A Discord thread (Sept 2025, link to be added) also reported that the HRE's Imperial Authority effects need a Victor; treat that as 🟡 until linked.
- **How many Ducats?** Still not stated for negotiated deals. Likely player-agreed, but there is no explicit ruling ([Q10](../../docs/open-questions.md)). Ducats in the same deal are settled together ([PR-017](017-negotiated-peace-ducats-settled-together.md)).

## Why (the intent)
Inconclusive wars are a bargain, not a verdict. The rules deliberately give the Victor-style menu only to someone who has earned it (2:1) or been conceded it (Surrender).
