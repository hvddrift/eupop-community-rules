# NPR counter-invasion, Rebels and Automatic White Peace

## Setup
Morocco had **vassalized Algiers**, then attacked the NPR **Tunis** and lost. Tunis then invaded Morocco and "liberated" Algiers.

## The question
Is the war an Automatic White Peace, or does Algiers return to Morocco under a "return to pre-war conditions" White Peace?

## Answer (🟢 Mikhs)
- **NPRs never Occupy Provinces.** A successful NPR Invasion **leaves Rebels**, which act as normal Rebels from then on.
- Algiers becomes independent through the Rebels (a Liberation). It does not go to Tunis.
- The war ends in an **Automatic White Peace**, which **does not change the board state**. Algiers stays independent and **the Rebels remain** (Miroku20X6 confirmed).
- Morocco loses Algiers and also loses **Prestige** from the Automatic White Peace (each PR loses 1 per White Peace it enters, Player Aid B).

## The trap
"White Peace: return to pre-war conditions" is the **Peace Term** that is negotiated or enforced. An **Automatic** White Peace just ends the war where it stands.
