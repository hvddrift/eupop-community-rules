# Force Conversion and "Control"

## Setup
The Victor has the **Livonian Order** Vassalized and **Riga** occupied during Peace Resolution, and wants to Force Convert **Estonia and Livonia**.

## The question
Force Conversion needs the Victor to **Control all Provinces in the Loser's Capital Area**. Does a Vassal count as Controlled?

## Answer
1. 🟢 **Mikhs: no.** **Controlled means you Own it or Occupy it.** A Vassal's Province is neither.
2. The Victor only occupies the target NPR's **Capital**, and the rest of the Area belongs to the Victor's **Vassals**, so they don't Control the whole Area and can't enforce Force Conversion.
3. Lavaloon asked whether occupation plus Total Victory would be enough. No: the term still needs the whole Capital Area, whatever the victory level.

## Rulebook
- Force Conversion (p.31) needs Partial or Total Victory, "Victor must Control all Provinces in Loser's Capital Area", and different Religions.
- p.28: a Province is Owned only if it is Lawfully Owned **and** Controlled by the same Realm. Vassals aren't part of their Overlord's Realm in general (§11.4, p.33).

## Watch out
This is the opposite of the Peace Resolution rule that treats the **enemy's** Vassals as part of the enemy Realm. It does not turn your **own** Vassals into Provinces you Control.
