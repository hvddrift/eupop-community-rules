# Secure Desired Succession, and the Alliance limit

## What the rulebook says
- The term ends with "The Victor **may** then gain an Alliance with the Loser" (p.31).
- It also says to **ignore the Loser's Active Ally status**, and, if the Loser is an NPR, **all their other Wars end on White Peace terms**.
- A PR can hold **3 Alliance tokens** (+1 with the Cabinet Idea), and an NPR can be Allied to **one** PR (§11.3, p.32).

## The question
If the Victor forges an Alliance with a **PR** Loser, is the Loser forced into it? If the Loser is at the Alliance limit, do they remove a different Alliance?

## What the community said
🟡 Mikhs: "It would make sense that they would remove a different Alliance token." That is hedged; it is not a ruling. Nobody answered whether a PR Loser can refuse.
