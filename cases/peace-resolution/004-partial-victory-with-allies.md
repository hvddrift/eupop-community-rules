# Partial Victory over a PR that has an Active Ally

## Setup
Player A is at war with Player B (human PR). B has an Active Ally, NPR C.

## The question
A has the units to claim Partial Victory against C but not against B. Can A force terms on C?

## Resolution
1. **No.** Unless A has a Partial Victory against the PR Enemy (B), A has **not** achieved one against B's Active Allies either (§10.3).
2. If A **does** have Partial Victory against B, it also applies to all of B's Active Allies.
3. Peace is then enforced or negotiated **simultaneously** for B and C. **Only one Peace Term** may be chosen for all realms involved (§10.3).
4. For **Vassalization** and **Force Conversion**, the term's effects apply only to the Realms for which the Victor can also satisfy all the additional requirements. For the remaining Realms, treat it as a White Peace between the parties (with no Prestige loss), with Occupied Provinces returned to their Lawful Owners (§10.3).
