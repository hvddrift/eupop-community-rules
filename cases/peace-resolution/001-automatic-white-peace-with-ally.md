# Automatic White Peace when the enemy has an Active Ally

## Setup
| Realm | Role |
|---|---|
| Player A | PR |
| Player B | PR, at war with A |
| C | NPR, Active Ally of B (ally token on the Active side) |

A occupies none of B's or C's Provinces. B and C occupy none of A's.

## The question
A and B are in a stalemate. Does A have to make peace with C separately, and what happens to C?

## Resolution, step by step
1. Step 1 of Peace Resolution checks Automatic White Peace first. **Neither side occupies any of the other's Provinces** (Provinces of Vassals and Active Allies count too). The condition is met (§10.1 step 1, table p.29).
2. When checking, C is counted **as part of B's realm**. A cannot make a separate peace with C (§10.1 step 1).
3. The war ends immediately as a White Peace. A is now at peace with **B and C** (§10.3, Automatic White Peace).
4. **Prestige:** each PR loses **1 Prestige per White Peace it enters**. A and B each lose 1. C is an NPR, so nothing happens to it (Player Aid B, the "End all such Wars with White Peace terms" box).
5. Truce: A and B get a Truce. **C gets no Truce token** because Truces are never placed on Active Allies of former enemies (§10.4).
6. Aftermath: units in Neutral Areas relocate to the nearest Friendly Area (at no cost), B's ally token flips back to the **Alliance** side (§10.1 step 4), and allied units return to the General Supply (§10.1 step 4).

## Why (the intent)
Automatic White Peace is a "nothing happened" reset, so there are no winners to demand terms or split allies off.

## Status notes
From the rulebook text plus the official flow chart (which is where the Prestige loss is spelled out). The Truce on C is the one people may overlook. *Correction:* an earlier version of this case left out the Prestige loss.
