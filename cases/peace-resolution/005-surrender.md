# Surrender

## Setup
Player B is losing a war to Player A. A has not met any Victory requirements.

## The question
What does B get by surrendering, and what does it cost?

## Resolution, step by step
1. A player may voluntarily Surrender **even if the Enemy hasn't met Total or Partial Victory requirements**. This names the Enemy as Victor (§10.3 Surrendering).
2. B **immediately loses 2 Prestige** and A **gains 2** (§10.3).
3. A may enforce Peace **as if they had achieved a Partial Victory**. A must still end the war this Round, either by enforcing an applicable term or by agreeing a Negotiated Peace (§10.3).
4. So **Full Annexation is not available** after a Surrender (Partial Victory does not allow it; table p.29).
5. **Only human players can Surrender**, and a player who Surrenders is the Loser. The Victor must Enforce a Peace that they can meet the requirements for (Player Aid B, footnote 6).
6. Humiliation normally needs the Victor to occupy the Loser's Capital, **unless the Loser has Surrendered** (§10.3 Humiliation, table footnote p.31).

## Why (the intent)
Surrender lets a player cut their losses on their own terms, but at a Prestige cost.

## Status notes
Whether Surrender Prestige stacks with Humiliation's Prestige exchange is in [Q6](../../docs/open-questions.md). Optional Rule 3 ("No Surrender!", p.31) removes this option.
