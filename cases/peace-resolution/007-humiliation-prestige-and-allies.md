# Humiliation and Provinces of Active Allies

## Setup
A (Victor, Partial Victory) occupies B's Capital. B is a human PR. B's Active Ally is NPR C. A occupies:
- B's Provinces (including B's Capital) with total Tax Value **3**
- C's Provinces with total Tax Value **4**

## The question
How much Prestige does A score from Humiliation?

## Resolution, step by step
1. Eligibility: Partial or Total Victory; Loser is a PR (B ✔); Victor occupies Loser's Capital or Loser Surrendered (✔). Humiliation is not available against NPRs or when you only hold an Inconclusive Result (table p.31).
2. **All Occupied Provinces are returned** to their Lawful Owners (p.30).
3. A scores Prestige = **2 × Tax Value of Provinces returned to the Loser**, max 10. B gets back Tax Value 3: 2 × 3 = **6 Prestige**.
4. B **loses the same 6 Prestige** (p.30).
5. Provinces returned to **C (the ally)** give **no Prestige**: A scores nothing for the Tax-4 Provinces (p.30; §10.3 says Active Ally Provinces must be returned without any Prestige reward).

## Status notes
The Tax Values are invented for the arithmetic, not taken from the board. Please replace them with a real example. Scoring 10 or more requires returning Tax Value 5+ to the Loser (the cap is 10).
