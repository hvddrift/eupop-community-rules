# Total Victory against an Active Ally NPR

## Setup
| Realm | Role |
|---|---|
| Player A | PR |
| Player B | PR, at war with A |
| C | NPR, Active Ally of B. Pre-war NPR Strength **6** |

A occupies all of C's *de jure* Provinces, and C has no Deployed Land Units (an NPR always counts as having 0 Deployed Land Units, §10.2). A has **not** won outright against B.

## The question
Can A end the war with C without ending it with B?

## Resolution, step by step
1. When determining Total Victory, an enemy's Active Allies are **treated separately** from the PR (table p.29, §10.3). A meets Total Victory against C.
2. Total Victory allows a **separate peace with Active Allies** (table p.29).
3. A resolves this in step 2 of Peace Resolution, in turn order, before any Partial Victories (§10.1). A must enforce a term of their choice unless a Negotiated Peace is agreed, which is not possible with an NPR (term table p.31).
4. Once peace with C is resolved, **B must discard Allied Units equal to ½ of C's pre-war strength: 3 units** (½ × 6). The war token is removed from the board (§10.3).
5. A and B are still at war (unless they separately resolve it).

## Why (the intent)
Beating the ally breaks the alliance and costs the PR the ally's units.

## Status notes
Straight from §10.3. The rounding for an odd pre-war strength is not stated: see future question.
